Research

I am a homotopy theorist by training and I am broadly interested in higher algebra. The bulk of my work is in a field known as tensor-triangular geometry. My work has explored both the foundations of tensor-triangular geometry as well as its applications to examples in chromatic homotopy theory, algebraic geometry, and modular representation theory. As such, I have played interloper among each of these crowds, and am always eager to learn more.
